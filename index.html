About HACKMMD

A modern interface for a smarter academic workflow.

HACKMMD is a software concept designed around an academic assistance workspace that may be used alongside configured environments such as Safe Exam Browser where permitted.

The platform is built to give users a simple place to understand the application, manage their account, review available packages, and contact support for activation and compatibility information.

Any functionality available inside an examination environment must always follow the rules of the institution, examination provider and the configured Safe Exam Browser environment.
